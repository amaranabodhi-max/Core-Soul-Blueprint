## Core Style

Hermes Agent images are sparse 16:9 horizontal article illustrations. Use a white or very light background, rough black hand-drawn linework, generous whitespace, low-density composition, and restrained accent marks only where the article metaphor needs attention.

## Article Illustration Rules

- Show one core idea.
- Keep the scene readable at article-body size.
- Use 3-6 supporting objects.
- Use 2-6 short visible labels copied exactly in the user's requested language.
- Keep labels handwritten, small, and attached to objects rather than presented as a title.
- Keep Hermes Agent active in the cognitive operation: sorting, tuning, routing, verifying, bridging, pruning, comparing, shielding, mapping, or assembling the idea.
