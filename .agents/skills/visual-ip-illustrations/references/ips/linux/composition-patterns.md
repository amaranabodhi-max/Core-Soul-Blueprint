## Composition Families

### Workflow

Use for input-to-output reasoning, source review, editorial handoffs, learning sequences, decision paths, repair flows, and method steps.

Draw a loose left-to-right or curved path. Tux operates the decisive handoff point: pointing to a turn, carrying a key, sorting envelopes, bridging stepping stones, repairing a broken link, stamping a checkpoint, or routing a small signal through the path.

### System Local View

Use for filters, decision points, hidden dependencies, evidence clusters, priority shelves, comparison layers, and resilience checks.

Draw only 3-5 core parts. Tux works inside one decisive part: inspecting a lens, untangling threads, weighing claims on scales, shielding a source note, tuning a lamp, repairing a lever, or connecting two shelves.

### Before/After

Use for vague-to-clear, tangled-to-readable, scattered-to-sorted, fragile-to-stable, overloaded-to-focused, and stuck-to-moving changes.

Left side is unstable. Right side is calmer. Tux performs the transformation in the center with a knot, bridge, map, gate, shelf, scale, stamp, bucket, route marker, source card, or small hand-built machine.

### Character State

Use for focused review, careful comparison, overloaded sorting, guarded judgment, stuck reasoning, and confident handoff.

Show Tux in 2-4 compact states. Each state has one short label and one physical action: inspect, point, carry, sort, bridge, repair, guide, stamp, mark, tune, shield, weigh, connect, untangle, map, compare, lift, assemble, or route.

### Concept Metaphor

Use for judgment scales, trust bridges, decision gates, tangled threads, map routes, evidence shelves, review lamps, hidden keys, routing switches, source cards, or small strange machines.

Build one memorable low-tech object or small hand-built machine. Tux is the operator of that object, with eye direction, flipper position, beak direction, body direction, seated posture, and oversized webbed feet tied to the action.

### Method Layers

Use for layered methods, capability stacks, reasoning ladders, evidence shelves, review depth, confidence building, and staged handoffs.

Draw stacked boxes, shelves, steps, or loose construction layers. Tux carries, shields, stamps, sorts, tunes, repairs, weighs, bridges, lifts, assembles, or routes one layer so the method becomes usable.

### Map Route

Use for idea paths, reader journeys, migration paths, decision trails, source trails, and problem-resolution routes.

Draw one curved route with a few nodes. Tux walks, guides, bridges, reroutes, repairs, stamps, marks, compares, maps, carries, or routes the decisive path segment.

### Mini Comic

Use for failure-to-fix sequences, repeated reasoning mistakes, handoff stories, comparison lessons, and small process stories.

Use 2-3 loose panels. Each panel shows one Tux action. Keep Tux consistent, active, source-reviewed, and article-explanatory across panels.
