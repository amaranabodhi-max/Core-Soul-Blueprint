# Herbarium of the Forgotten Witch — Page I: HOLLOWBELL
Vertical 9:16, ~60 s. English final text. On-page text is added in CapCut (animated handwriting), never in the prompt.

## Anchors (use in every prompt)
- STYLE TAIL: painterly cinematic, dark folk fairytale, Lithuanian village, soft film grain, 4K, highly detailed, vertical 9:16
- PALETTE: warm candle gold against deep indigo shadows
- FLOWER: a single hollow bell-shaped flower on a slender bent stem, silver rim deepening to teal and indigo inside, no stamens, faint glow at its empty heart
- HEROINE (old): old herbalist woman, grey-green eyes, single silver braid, small silver ring on her left hand
- HEROINE (young): young woman, the same grey-green eyes, single dark braid, the same small silver ring on her left hand
- TOMAS: young man at a wooden gate, translucent, dissolving like mist

## 1. Hook (0–3 s)
Witch (to camera): "I wrote this warning. I don't remember writing it."
Prompt: Extreme close-up, [HEROINE old], her finger resting on a pressed Hollowbell flower between the pages of an aged herbarium, blank margin with faded handwriting; the dried bell slowly turns toward her and its hollow heart begins to glow teal; she lifts her eyes to the camera. 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, slow push-in, single candle, [PALETTE], hushed, uncanny, intimate, [STYLE TAIL]
Alt opening: camera glides over the herbarium page first, the flower stirs, only then her eyes rise.

## 2. The herbarium (3–12 s)
Veyra (V.O.): "Some poisons don't kill. They erase."
Prompt: Macro shot of an aged herbarium page, ink-and-watercolour drawing of [FLOWER] beside the real pressed flower, blank margin with faded handwriting, candlelight flickering across the paper. 100mm macro, slow pan, [PALETTE], [STYLE TAIL]
CapCut overlay: "Oblivaria cava" (label) and margin note "Don't call it until you remember." appearing as handwriting.

## 3. The plant (12–25 s)
Veyra (V.O.): "Hollowbell grows where the ground remembers footsteps. Healers sought it for grief. But call it by another's name, and you forget your own."
Prompt: Misty night, an old village path worn deep by footsteps leading to a wooden gate, moonrise, a few [FLOWER] along the path glowing faintly. 35mm lens, slow dolly forward, moonlight and indigo mist, [STYLE TAIL]

## 4. The past (25–40 s)
4A (25–29 s) Prompt: Warm amber flashback, [HEROINE young] kneels on the moonlit path holding a Hollowbell, tears on her cheeks; at the gate stands [TOMAS], the night visible through him. 50mm lens, slow push-in, amber against indigo, [STYLE TAIL]
4B (~29–30.5 s, separate shot) Prompt: [HEROINE young] suddenly turns and looks directly into the camera for a moment, as if seeing someone watching from the future. 85mm lens, static, amber light, [STYLE TAIL]
4C (30.5–40 s) Girl whispers: "Tomas…" Veyra (V.O.): "She gave it his name. It took hers."
Prompt: She whispers into the flower, the bell closes around her words, a thin silver smoke drifts from her temple and fades; the gate is now empty. 50mm lens, slow pull-back, amber fading to indigo, [STYLE TAIL]

## 5. The turn (40–52 s)
Witch: "You remember him. You remember me."
5A Prompt: Present day, [HEROINE old] touches the pressed Hollowbell in the herbarium; the dried bell opens on the paper toward her and glows teal. 85mm lens, slow push-in, single candle, [PALETTE], [STYLE TAIL]
5B Prompt: Extreme macro of a single tear falling onto the pressed silver petal, reflecting the old herbalist's face with grey-green eyes. 100mm macro, static, single candle, [PALETTE], [STYLE TAIL]
5C Prompt: Extreme macro of the same tear on the pressed petal, now reflecting a young woman's face with the same grey-green eyes and dark braid. 100mm macro, static, [STYLE TAIL]
Edit: soft crossfade 5B -> 5C -> 5B. (The tear is why the bell opens: Hollowbell opens after someone has wept nearby — as she once wept at the gate.)

## 6. Final (52–60 s)
Witch: "Next page… next name."  Veyra (V.O.): "See you on Monday."
Prompt: She turns the herbarium page to a blank sheet; a new botanical drawing of an unknown plant slowly bleeds through the paper in ink, candle flickers, fade to black. 50mm lens, static, [PALETTE], [STYLE TAIL]

## Plant card (reference)
HOLLOWBELL, Oblivaria cava; folk names: Widow's Lantern, the Nameless Bell, Užmarštė. Grows only on paths walked so often the ground remembers footsteps. Opens at moonrise for one night after someone has wept nearby. Gift: keeps a lost one's name and quiets grief. Poison: holds only one name; give it another's and it takes yours. Sign: a pressed Hollowbell holding a stolen name stays faintly warm and opens only for the one whose name it took.
