## Plant card (reference)
STILLFACE, Persona pallida; folk names: the Calm Face, the Wife's Mask, Lady's Quiet.
The order: a young wife (no name, no title) in a great house where every flicker of her face was carried to others. She wanted a face that would never give her away. Madame de Velmont set one Stillface into a silver-gilt mount. It ended well for the house and badly for the wife: the calm outlasted the feeling, and she wore it until she could not remember what her face did when she was glad. The witch tried the mount on her own cheek, only to be sure; it kept her laugh.
Grows on north faces of old stone walls that no sun has ever looked at. Gift: holds a trembling face quiet through any hour. Poison: the calm outlasts the feeling, and joy cannot find its way back to the face. Sign: a pressed Stillface never stirs in a draught; if it holds a stolen laugh, a faint laughing face shows in its surface for the one it took from.
Takes: her laugh.
