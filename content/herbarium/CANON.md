# Herbarium of the Forgotten Witch — Canon

The shared canon of the series. It does not change from page to page. The Monday agent reads this file and `INDEX.md` before every new page.

- **World:** dark folk fairytale, a Lithuanian village, candle and moon.
- **Heroine:** an old herbalist who does not remember that she wrote the herbarium herself. Every page is a plant tied to her forgotten life.
- **Voice:** Veyra off-screen, as guide and decoder, not the heroine.
- **Palette:** warm candle gold against deep indigo; teal is the colour of the plants' magic.
- **Style tail:** `painterly cinematic, dark folk fairytale, Lithuanian village, soft film grain, 4K, highly detailed, vertical 9:16`
- **Heroine in prompts:** old herbalist woman, grey-green eyes, single silver braid, small silver ring on her left hand (in youth: the same eyes and ring, dark braid).
- **Text on herbarium pages** (Latin names, margin notes) is **never written in the prompt**, only `blank margin with faded handwriting`. Ruta adds the real text in CapCut.
- **Ending of every video:** a blank page, a new plant bleeds through, *"See you on Monday."*
- **Page structure:** anchors → scenes with timing, lines and prompts → plant card.
- **All channel text is in English.**
