## 1. The rival physician and antimony
**Status:** idea · added 2026-10-26

**The story.** A learned physician comes to town, one of the new doctors who healed with metals: antimony and mercury. He thinks Madame de Velmont is a witch; she thinks he is a poisoner.

**The rhyme.** Her glass lamp tests the truth (Dusklantern); his metal cup turns wine into poison.

**Truth of the period for the hook:**
- **"The new doctors who healed with metals"** belong to the 16th century. This is the wording for episodes.
- Reference only, never said or shown in videos: these doctors followed Paracelsus (1493–1541).
- **Antimony cups and "everlasting pills"** belong to the 17th century. Use them carefully and never present them as 1551.
- **Kohl:** a dark eye powder made from antimony or lead. The white faces and black brows of rich ladies are the same poison.

**The plant for this episode** must stand against metal. It is invented, like every plant in the series.

**Ruta's decision (2026-10-26):** Paracelsus is never named in videos. Episodes say "the new doctors who healed with metals"; his name lives only in this file, as a reference.

**For discussion. The lady's fate is open; nothing here goes into the canon yet:**
- **Option A, possible:** the physician is the one who accused Madame de Velmont when the lady fell asleep from Bride's Shadow.
- **Option B, to discuss:** the lady did not die. She slept from Bride's Shadow, and perhaps she woke in our time before the heroine did.
