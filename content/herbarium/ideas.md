## 1. The rival physician and antimony
**Status:** idea · added 2026-10-26

**The story.** A learned physician comes to town, a follower of Paracelsus. He treats with metals: antimony and mercury. He thinks Madame de Velmont is a witch; she thinks he is a poisoner. Perhaps he is the one who accused her when the lady fell asleep from Bride's Shadow.

**The rhyme.** Her glass lamp tests the truth (Dusklantern); his metal cup turns wine into poison.

**Truth of the period for the hook:**
- **Paracelsus and "metallic healing"** belong to the 16th century.
- **Antimony cups and "everlasting pills"** belong to the 17th century. Use them carefully and never present them as 1551.
- **Kohl:** a dark eye powder made from antimony or lead. The white faces and black brows of rich ladies are the same poison.

**The plant for this episode** must stand against metal. It is invented, like every plant in the series.

**Check before use:**
- Paracelsus is a real historical figure, and the canon forbids real historical figures. Here he would only be mentioned as a teacher, never shown. Ruta decides whether a mention is allowed or whether to say "the new metal doctors" without his name.
- If the physician is the accuser, that touches the secret canon and the open thread about the lady. Decide this before the episode is written.
