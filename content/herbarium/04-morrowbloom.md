## Plant card (reference)
MORROWBLOOM, Somnium aurorae; folk names: the Dream-Keeper, Sleeper's Dawn, the Morning Thief. Grows on cold moorland among birches and old wall-stones, and opens for one hour before sunrise. The dew on it runs upward. Gift: keeps the night's dream from fading at sunrise. Poison: a kept dream is paid for with the keeper's own; the nights go blank.

**The order:** an old man (client by role, no name or title) dreamed the same lit house on a shore every night and forgot the face at its window every morning. He wanted to wake with it.
- What she made: a petal set into amber and brass, the Dawn Pane. Named only, never a recipe.
- How it ended: he woke with every dream, slept longer each day, and his own nights went blank. She learned the price in her own sleep.
- How it answers her: at dawn it unfolds toward her, and shows a dream of hers it kept: someone at a lit window, face turned away. Whose face stays an open thread.
- Takes: her dreams.
