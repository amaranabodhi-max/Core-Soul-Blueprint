## Plant card
RUSTVEIL, Ferrugo velata. A pale, almost translucent flower like lichen, growing only on old iron: rusted nails, graveyard railings, hinges of abandoned gates. Overnight it turns metal to red dust.

**The order:** a noble mother whose daughter was getting worse from a physician's metal cure.

- Gift: draws metal poison out of the one who drank it.
- Poison: the warmth leaves with the metal.
- Takes: the warmth of her hands.
